### Adjudication C-05 — validation versus selection and pooling

- **Original:** “The Phase-21 global reference achieved a frozen validation result.”
- **Primary locations:** `reports/audits/phase21_final_freeze.json`: `training.seed`, `training.best_epoch`, `training.best_macro_domain_dice`, `results.global_medfilm.macro_domain_dice`, `canonical_val_evaluation`; `reports/tables/phase21_global_medfilm_domain_performance.csv`: `MACRO_DOMAIN` and `POOLED_DESCRIPTIVE` rows, `dice` and `note`; `reports/audits/phase21_protocol_binding.json`: inherited selection/metric contract.
- **Scientific problem:** Original sentence is numerically compatible but underspecifies selection reuse and estimator. Canonical scoring and checkpoint-selection values are different; pixel-pooled Dice is not equal-domain macro. Existence of scored validation predictions is not independent validation.
- **Corrected claim:** The single-seed, VAL-selected GlobalMedFiLM checkpoint has canonical VAL808 equal-domain macro Dice 0.8350794169575282; its selection macro was 0.8350791259413984. Pixel-pooled descriptive Dice 0.8784243406054913 is separately labelled, not substituted.
- **Population/estimand:** VAL808 (ISIC 357, BUSI 84, KVASIR 87, KUB 280); mean per-image Dice within each domain followed by equal four-domain weighting; selection seed 42/epoch 66. These are recorded aggregates, not a new recomputation.
- **Classification:** EXECUTED_DESCRIPTIVE; DEVELOPMENT_SELECTED/CONSUMED_VALIDATION.
- **Limitations/final status:** One global training seed, no architecture-matched control or untouched confirmation. **DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY; C1 attribution NOT_ESTABLISHED.**
