# RA-MedFiLM-BARR Claims-to-Evidence Matrix

## Use rules

This matrix preserves the source artifact's evidence boundary. **FROZEN_AUTHORITY** denotes a binding specification, architecture, manifest, scope, or immutable result record. **EXECUTED_DESCRIPTIVE** denotes a completed measurement with a stated protocol and population, not a confirmatory or generalization claim. **EXPLORATORY** denotes a bounded observation that is not primary confirmatory evidence. **PROSPECTIVE** denotes a planned or requested study. **UNRESOLVED** means a required claim lacks adequate evidence. **SUPERSEDED** identifies a record that is not controlling where a later correction applies. No claim below is based on new training, inference, TEST access, ROUTER_HOLDOUT access, or reanalysis performed for this draft.

## Mandatory manuscript wording

Use “descriptive development evidence,” “frozen implementation,” “conditional regional refinement,” and “segmentation-harm” where applicable. Do not use “FiLM caused,” “clinically safe,” “superior,” “protocol-faithful baseline comparison,” “independent generalization,” or “BARR-M1 executed.” Quantitative statements must retain the source, population, estimand, and evidence class shown above.
